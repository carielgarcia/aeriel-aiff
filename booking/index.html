Intent

The dancefloor is an ephemeral sanctuary built on sweat, friction and desire. On a dancefloor, individual sovereignty and collective freedom can coexist; safer spaces can be built and sustained. It is a space where we can reclaim, even if briefly, our body and desire. I reject the idea of joy as resistance, but dancefloors have shown that other ways of organising and living together are possible. As a DJ, I am honoured to help build and look after them.

— ÆRIEL

Bio

ÆRIEL (They/Them – He/Him) is a non-binary, queer selector based in Santiago de Chile. Their sets cross techno, trance, EBM, house and Italo disco, changing tempo and temperature over the course of a night.

ÆRIEL lived in Paris and has spent long stretches in São Paulo and Buenos Aires, where friends, lovers and family keep pulling them back. Each city has shaped how they approach the night. Paris showed them that counterculture can be elegant, and that spontaneity and genuine creation are worth pursuing. São Paulo's vast queer underground proved that artistic excellence, eroticism and political conviction share the dancefloor and make for bombastic nights. Buenos Aires added irreverence: a punk, bohemian hunger for breaking with whatever has become the norm. ÆRIEL brings all three back to Santiago and wherever they go.

In April 2026, ÆRIEL founded Æ000.X, an invitation-only party in Santiago where they serve as conceptual lead. Æ000.X builds an intentional community around music, counterculture and desire. It is a direct response to the gentrification and commodification of techno, run on principle, care and experimentation. Each edition is curated as a whole, from the guest list to the music, and the party is the clearest picture of how ÆRIEL thinks a night should be held.

Away from the decks, they write essays, photograph nightlife on 35mm film and Polaroid, and have a boring day job.

Santiago de Chile

Technical rider & booking

[ Technical rider ]

Preferred setup

2× Pioneer DJ CDJ-3000 and a Pioneer DJ mixer.

Minimum

Any equipment that runs Rekordbox, with a Pioneer DJ DDJ-FLX4 as the minimum. If the controller needs a computer, a MacBook with an M-series chip is preferred.

Sound system

Hi-fi sound is always appreciated.

Sound check

ÆRIEL pays special attention to volume, clipping and bass saturation. If possible, a sound check in advance keeps the sound crisp, clear and unclipped, and keeps it from hurting the ears of those in attendance.

[ Booking requirements ]

Hospitality

ÆRIEL does not drink alcohol. Requested: 2× Red Bull (sugar-free or Zero), 1× isotonic drink and 1× Coca-Cola Zero.

Stage and access

ÆRIEL prefers no VIP areas. If people dance behind the decks, access is equal for all partygoers and nobody touches the equipment. If the DJ stage is VIP regardless, ÆRIEL requires zero contact and no photographs with the people around them.

Intención

La pista de baile es un santuario efímero hecho de sudor, fricción y deseo. En ella pueden coexistir la soberanía individual y la libertad colectiva, y es posible construir y sostener espacios más seguros. Es un lugar donde podemos recuperar, aunque sea por un momento, nuestro cuerpo y nuestro deseo. Rechazo la idea del goce como resistencia, pero las pistas de baile han demostrado que existen otras formas de organizarnos y de convivir. Como DJ, me honra ayudar a construirlas y cuidarlas.

— ÆRIEL

Bio

ÆRIEL (Elle – Él) es un selector queer no binario con base en Santiago de Chile. Sus sets recorren techno, trance, EBM, house e Italo disco, y cambian de tempo y de temperatura a lo largo de la noche.

ÆRIEL vivió en París y ha pasado largas temporadas en São Paulo y Buenos Aires, donde amistades, amantes y familia siguen haciéndole volver. Cada ciudad ha marcado su forma de entender la noche. París le enseñó que la contracultura puede ser elegante y que vale la pena buscar la espontaneidad y la creación genuina. El vasto underground queer de São Paulo demostró que la excelencia artística, el erotismo y la convicción política pueden compartir la pista y producir noches explosivas. Buenos Aires sumó irreverencia: un hambre punk y bohemia de romper con lo que se ha convertido en norma. ÆRIEL trae las tres consigo de vuelta a Santiago y adonde vaya.

En abril de 2026, ÆRIEL fundó Æ000.X, una fiesta en Santiago solo con invitación, de la cual es líder conceptual. Æ000.X construye una comunidad intencional en torno a la música, la contracultura y el deseo. Responde de forma directa a la gentrificación y la mercantilización del techno, y se sostiene sobre principios, cuidado y experimentación. Cada edición se cura como un todo, desde la lista de invitadxs hasta la música, y la fiesta es la imagen más clara de cómo ÆRIEL cree que debe sostenerse una noche.

Fuera de la cabina, escribe ensayos, fotografía la noche en película de 35 mm y Polaroid, y tiene un trabajo diurno aburrido.

Santiago de Chile
