PRIVACY

ÆRIEL counts visits without cookies, without accounts and without third-party scripts that follow people to other sites. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control (Sec-GPC: 1), only an anonymous counter is added: no visit record and no form metadata are stored.

Daily countersSums per day: sessions, device type, referrer family, views, essays opened, reading progress, sets opened, clicks to Instagram, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and YouTube, language, theme, time on page in ranges.Understand what is used.No limit (sums, not people).
