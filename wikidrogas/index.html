EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

First Aid & Dancefloor Emergency Protocol

What to do if someone becomes unresponsive, overheats, or enters a difficult psychological state.

1

1. Check Responsiveness

Gently tap their shoulder and speak clearly. If unresponsive, check breathing and immediately call medical staff or emergency services (112 / 911 / 192).

2

2. The Recovery Position

If they are breathing but unconscious (e.g., G-sleep or Ketamine sedation), place them in the stable recovery position on their side to prevent choking on vomit.

3

3. Overheating & Hyperthermia

Hot skin, stopped sweating, confusion, and muscle stiffness are red flags. Move immediately to a cool area, loosen tight clothing, and fan cool air.

4

4. Compassionate Grounding

For panic or overwhelming psychedelic trips: stay calm, reassure them they are safe, breathe together in four-second intervals, and never leave them alone.

About Wikidrogas

Wikidrogas is an independent harm reduction knowledge repository hosted on the ÆRIEL platform. It provides dancers, ravers, and community members with accessible, evidence-based safety data grounded in toxicology and frontline peer support.

This wiki is for educational and harm reduction purposes only. It does not promote or encourage substance use. Using unsanctioned psychoactive substances always carries inherent health risks.
